Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

GeoAlarm is a location-based alarm app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how GeoAlarm handles information when you use the app.

Information GeoAlarm uses

GeoAlarm uses location permission to detect when your device is near a destination you selected for an alarm. Location processing for alarms happens on your device.

GeoAlarm may store alarm information locally on your device, such as alarm names, destination coordinates, radius settings, schedules, and app preferences. This information is used to provide the app's alarm features.

Information GeoAlarm does not collect

  • GeoAlarm does not upload your live location to a GeoAlarm server.
  • GeoAlarm does not upload saved destination coordinates, alarm names, radius settings, or schedule details to a GeoAlarm server.
  • GeoAlarm does not sell your personal information.

Location permission

GeoAlarm asks for location permission so it can monitor whether you enter the alarm area. Background location permission may be requested so alarms can work when the app is not open. You can change or revoke location permission at any time in Android system settings.

Google Maps Platform

GeoAlarm uses Google Maps Platform services, including Google Maps SDK for Android and Places SDK, to display maps and help you search for places. When you use map or place search features, information such as search queries, selected place data, device information, and usage data may be processed by Google according to Google's policies.

For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

GeoAlarm may show ads using Google Mobile Ads. Google Mobile Ads may process information such as your device information, advertising identifier, approximate location, app interactions, and ad interactions to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads.

GeoAlarm does not send saved destination coordinates, alarm names, radius settings, schedule details, or place search text to Google Mobile Ads as ad targeting data.

Where required, GeoAlarm shows Google's consent and privacy options before requesting ads. You can manage ad privacy options in the app when this option is available. For more information, review the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

GeoAlarm may use notifications to show active alarm monitoring status and alert you when an alarm is triggered. Notification permission can be managed in Android system settings.

Data retention

Alarm data and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, reset app data, or uninstall the app. Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google's policies.

GeoAlarm does not include locally stored alarm data or app preferences in Android cloud backup. If you use Android device-to-device transfer during phone migration, Android may copy GeoAlarm app data directly from your old device to your new device.

Children's privacy

GeoAlarm is not directed to children under 13. GeoAlarm does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 24 日

GeoAlarm 是一款 Android 定位鬧鐘應用程式。本隱私權政策說明您使用 GeoAlarm 時，應用程式如何處理資訊。

GeoAlarm 使用的資訊

GeoAlarm 會使用定位權限，判斷您的裝置是否接近您為鬧鐘選擇的目的地。鬧鐘所需的定位判斷會在您的裝置上處理。

GeoAlarm 可能會在您的裝置本機儲存鬧鐘資訊，例如鬧鐘名稱、目的地座標、半徑設定、排程和應用程式偏好設定。這些資訊用於提供定位鬧鐘功能。

GeoAlarm 不會收集的資訊

  • GeoAlarm 不會將您的即時定位上傳到 GeoAlarm 伺服器。
  • GeoAlarm 不會將已儲存的目的地座標、鬧鐘名稱、半徑設定或排程細節上傳到 GeoAlarm 伺服器。
  • GeoAlarm 不會出售您的個人資訊。

定位權限

GeoAlarm 會要求定位權限，以便監控您是否進入鬧鐘範圍。為了讓鬧鐘在 app 未開啟時也能運作，GeoAlarm 可能會要求背景定位權限。您可以隨時在 Android 系統設定中變更或撤回定位權限。

Google Maps Platform

GeoAlarm 使用 Google Maps Platform 服務，包括 Google Maps SDK for Android 和 Places SDK，用於顯示地圖及協助您搜尋地點。當您使用地圖或地點搜尋功能時，搜尋字詞、所選地點資料、裝置資訊和使用資料等資訊可能會依 Google 的政策由 Google 處理。

更多資訊請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。

廣告

GeoAlarm 可能會使用 Google Mobile Ads 顯示廣告。Google Mobile Ads 可能會處理裝置資訊、廣告識別碼、約略位置、app 互動和廣告互動等資訊，用於提供、限制、衡量和改善廣告。

GeoAlarm 不會將已儲存的目的地座標、鬧鐘名稱、半徑設定、排程細節或地點搜尋文字作為廣告指定目標資料傳送給 Google Mobile Ads。

在需要的地區，GeoAlarm 會在請求廣告前顯示 Google 的同意和隱私選項。當 app 提供此選項時，您可以在 app 中管理廣告隱私選項。更多資訊請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。

通知

GeoAlarm 可能會使用通知來顯示正在監控的鬧鐘狀態，並在鬧鐘觸發時提醒您。您可以在 Android 系統設定中管理通知權限。

資料保留

儲存在本機的鬧鐘資料和偏好設定會保留在您的裝置上，直到您刪除資料、重設 app 資料或解除安裝 app。廣告資料會依 Google 自身政策保留。

GeoAlarm 不會將儲存在本機的鬧鐘資料或應用程式偏好設定納入 Android 雲端備份。若您在更換手機時使用 Android 裝置對裝置轉移功能，Android 可能會將 GeoAlarm app 資料直接從舊裝置複製到新裝置。

兒童隱私

GeoAlarm 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為主要對象。GeoAlarm 不會故意收集兒童的個人資訊。

政策變更

本隱私權政策可能會不定期更新。更新後的內容會發布於本頁，並標示新的生效日期。
