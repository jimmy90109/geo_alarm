GeoAlarm

A location-based alarm app for Android. GeoAlarm helps you set reminders that ring when you arrive near a selected destination.

Privacy Policy Samsung Now Bar guide Source Code

Privacy at a glance

Location data is used on the device to provide location-based alarms. GeoAlarm does not upload your live location, saved destinations, alarm names, radius settings, or schedule details to a GeoAlarm server.

The app may use Google Maps Platform services for maps and place search, and optional anonymous analytics to understand app stability and usage.

Samsung Now Bar setup

On Samsung One UI 8, third-party live alarm updates may need an advanced system setting before they can appear in Now Bar.
